SPROUT TAMERS

farm · befriend · breed · defend

Grow seasonal crops side by side to attract 16 plant-spirit sprouts. Befriend them, breed them for traits, let them water, harvest and guard your farm, and fight back the blight in light turn-based battles.

▶ Play in browser
Free · no install · mouse, keyboard or touch · progress saves automatically

What you do

Farm in four seasons

12 crops, 3 per season. One tap tills, plants, waters or harvests. Watered crops grow each night.

Crop combos attract sprouts

Two ripe crops side by side (Dewbean + Mossleaf, Lanternkin + Mistgrape...) draw one of 16 sprouts overnight.

Sprouts work your farm

Waterers water plots, Harvesters gather ripe crops, Guardians chase off blight mites at night.

Befriend & breed

Pet them daily and share Sweet Nectar. Two sprouts with 6+ friendship breed a seedling that inherits a trait from each parent.

Blight battles

Turn-based fights in the Wild Woods with skills, SP and elements. Each season ends with a blight boss attacking the farm.

Town & festival

Tilly's market, Bram's workshop, Juniper's clinic, Mayor Olwen's requests, and a festival with ribbons after every boss.
